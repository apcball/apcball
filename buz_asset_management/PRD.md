# PRD.md --- BUZ Asset Management for Odoo 17

**Status:** Draft for implementation\
**Technical name:** `buz_asset_management`\
**Platform:** Odoo 17.0\
**Scope:** Full Asset Management\
**Depreciation:** Accounting depreciation only; tax depreciation is out
of scope.

## 1. Product vision

Build an auditable fixed-asset subledger and operational asset register
inside Odoo 17. The module connects physical assets to acquisition
evidence and accounting entries, while giving Finance, IT, Admin and
department managers a shared lifecycle view.

The module is not a second general ledger. `account.move` and
`account.move.line` remain the authoritative accounting records. BUZ
asset records manage physical identity, custody, location, lifecycle
events and links to accounting sources.

### Goals

1.  Register assets from Vendor Bills or manually.
2.  Maintain one traceable record per individually controlled asset,
    with optional parent/component relationships.
3.  Calculate and schedule accounting depreciation using approved
    company policy.
4.  Create, post, reverse and reconcile depreciation entries through
    Odoo Accounting.
5.  Track assignment, location, transfer, audit, maintenance, warranty
    and disposal.
6.  Provide audit history and reports for Finance and Asset Control.
7.  Support multi-company, multi-currency, Thai/English labels and
    responsive Odoo views.

### Non-goals for v1

-   Tax depreciation or tax-return computation.
-   Replacing Purchase, Inventory, Accounting, Fleet, Maintenance or HR.
-   Automatic stock moves for every asset movement.
-   Posting outside Odoo accounting models.
-   Automatic capitalization without an approved category/policy.
-   Leasing/IFRS 16, impairment engine, revaluation engine or full
    component accounting. These may be future phases.
-   Posting through accounting lock dates or company date controls.

## 3. Functional requirements

### 3.1 Asset register

Each asset includes: - Unique immutable asset code generated by
company/category sequence. - Name, category, description, image and
attachments. - Serial number, manufacturer, model, barcode/QR value. -
Company, currency, acquisition date, in-service date and depreciation
start date. - Vendor, Vendor Bill and Vendor Bill Line references when
acquired from a bill. - Original cost, approved capitalized additional
cost, residual value, useful life and depreciation method. - Fixed
asset, accumulated depreciation and expense account mappings, plus
journal (or reference to native asset configuration). - Department,
employee/custodian, location, parent asset and component
relationships. - Warranty, insurance and lifecycle history. -
Operational status, approval status and accounting status as separate
concepts.

Do not store a second editable copy of accounting balances. Derive book
value from posted accounting entries or the selected native Odoo asset
backend.

### 3.2 Asset creation sources

#### A. Vendor Bill

-   Provide an action on Vendor Bill / bill lines to create one or more
    assets.
-   Default to posted vendor bills for capitalization.
-   Validate company, currency, account, amount, partner and bill state.
-   Prevent duplicate capitalization of the same bill line or allocated
    amount.
-   Support splitting a bill line into multiple individually tracked
    assets. Sum of allocations must not exceed eligible line amount
    using currency rounding.
-   Support partial capitalization and preserve source bill-line
    reference.
-   Exclude tax, freight and other lines unless category policy
    explicitly permits allocation and Accounting approves it.
-   Use a wizard for allocation, category, asset count, per-unit amount,
    in-service date, department and location.
-   If native Odoo automation already created an asset from the line,
    extend/link that asset instead of creating a duplicate.

#### B. Manual registration

-   Allow authorized users to register assets without Vendor Bill
    (opening balances, donated assets, internally constructed assets or
    legacy assets).
-   Require source type, reason/reference, acquisition date, in-service
    date, original cost, currency, company, category, approval and
    supporting evidence.
-   Manual registration must not silently create an accounting entry. A
    separate capitalization/opening action must be approved and create a
    balanced `account.move`.
-   For opening assets, capture opening gross cost and accumulated
    depreciation, with an opening reconciliation workflow.

### 3.3 Asset categories

Category defaults: - Code prefix/sequence and tracking mode. - Default
company and accounts. - Depreciation method (straight-line for MVP),
useful life in months, frequency, start-date policy, prorata and
residual-value policy. - Journal, analytic distribution defaults and
department requirements. - Approval rules, maintenance/warranty defaults
and QR requirement.

Accounts must be company-specific and validated against account company
and account types. Never hardcode account codes.

### 3.4 Accounting depreciation

MVP supports accounting depreciation only.

Inputs: - Gross capitalized cost, residual value and depreciable base. -
Useful life / number of periods. - Depreciation frequency (monthly by
default). - Depreciation start date and prorata policy. - Company
currency and rounding. - Expense and accumulated depreciation
accounts. - Journal and optional analytic distribution.

Outputs: - Schedule with due date, planned amount, posted amount,
remaining amount, status and linked journal entry. - Recalculation
preview before approval. - Journal entry per period or approved batch,
per configuration. - Posted amount and book value derived from posted
accounting entries. - Traceable link from each schedule line to its
journal entry.

**Straight-line formula**

`depreciable_base = max(0, gross_cost - residual_value)`

`period_amount = depreciable_base / number_of_periods`

Round using Odoo currency rounding. Apply any rounding remainder to the
final period so total depreciation never exceeds the depreciable base.
If prorata temporis is enabled, define and test the day-count convention
and first/last period rules; do not assume a tax convention.

Posting: - Debit: Depreciation Expense - Credit: Accumulated
Depreciation

Accounts, journal, analytic distribution, date and labels come from
company/category/asset configuration. Use Odoo ORM and accounting APIs;
never insert accounting rows with SQL.

### 3.5 Accounting safeguards

-   Respect company lock dates, tax lock dates where relevant, journal
    restrictions and custom Accounting Date Policy controls.
-   Do not bypass posting validation or use privileged context flags to
    force-post.
-   If intended date is locked, block posting and explain the next
    permitted action.
-   Posting requires appropriate accounting group.
-   Use a unique idempotency key per asset + schedule line + action.
    Repeated cron runs/clicks must not create duplicate entries.
-   Posted moves are never deleted or silently edited. Corrections use
    reversal and a new adjustment/schedule.
-   Validate multi-company references.
-   Reconciliation compares asset gross cost, accumulated depreciation
    and NBV to GL balances, with explicit treatment of opening balances,
    disposals, adjustments and drafts.

### 3.6 Transfers and custody

Transfer record contains transfer number, asset, from/to employee,
department and location, effective date, requestor, approver,
acknowledgement, notes and attachments.

States: Draft → Submitted → Approved → Done; alternatives Rejected /
Cancelled.

On completion update current custody/location while preserving a
read-only historical event. Prevent overlapping active assignments.
Inter-company transfers are out of MVP and require a separate accounting
workflow.

### 3.7 Maintenance

-   Maintenance request/work log linked to asset.
-   Type: preventive, corrective, inspection, calibration or
    configurable.
-   Planned/actual dates, vendor/technician, cost, downtime,
    description, parts, attachments and result.
-   Status: Draft, Planned, In Progress, Done, Cancelled.
-   Warranty coverage flag.
-   Maintenance cost must not automatically increase asset cost;
    capitalization is a separate approved accounting decision.
-   Optional recurring reminders via activities.

### 3.8 Physical audit

-   Create campaigns by company, date, department, location or category.
-   Snapshot expected asset, custodian and location at campaign start.
-   Mobile QR/barcode scan records found/not found, actual
    location/custodian, condition, photo and notes.
-   Results: Not Checked, Found, Not Found, Wrong Location, Wrong
    Custodian, Damaged, Retired/Disposed.
-   Expected snapshot is immutable; observations must not overwrite it.
-   Approval resolves exceptions through transfer, correction,
    investigation or disposal.
-   Report completion rate and unresolved exceptions.

### 3.9 QR and labels

-   Generate stable QR payload using internal asset identifier or secure
    web route; do not encode confidential accounting data.
-   QR opens asset only for authorized users.
-   PDF labels include company, asset code, short name and QR/barcode.
-   Scanning works on supported mobile browsers and degrades gracefully
    without camera permission.

### 3.10 Warranty and insurance

Track provider, reference, start/end dates, terms, coverage, policy
number, insured amount and documents. Send configurable expiry
reminders. No automatic insurance accounting in MVP.

### 3.11 Disposal / sale / write-off

-   Request includes reason, proposed date, method (sale, scrap, loss,
    donation, other), proceeds, fees, buyer/customer, documents and
    approver.
-   Calculate NBV using posted depreciation and approved adjustments up
    to disposal date.
-   Generate balanced disposal move using configured accounts and Odoo
    accounting APIs.
-   For sale, link customer invoice/credit note where applicable; do not
    duplicate receivable or revenue.
-   Recognize gain/loss using configured accounts and correct NBV.
-   Locked disposal date blocks posting.
-   On completion mark operational status Disposed and stop future
    depreciation. Preserve history and posted moves.
-   Partial disposal/component derecognition is future enhancement
    unless approved.

## 4. State model

Keep operational, approval and accounting states separate.

**Asset lifecycle:**
`draft → submitted → approved → in_service → disposed`\
Additional operational states: `under_maintenance`, `missing`,
`on_loan`, `fully_depreciated`. Fully depreciated does not mean
physically disposed.

**Depreciation line:** `planned → draft_move → posted`; alternatives
`cancelled`, `reversed`, `exception`.

**Transfer:** `draft → submitted → approved → done`; alternatives
`rejected`, `cancelled`.

**Audit:** `draft → in_progress → submitted → approved → closed`;
alternative `cancelled`.

**Disposal:** `draft → submitted → approved → posted → done`;
alternatives `rejected`, `cancelled`.

All transitions must be explicit server-side methods with
permission/state validation.

## 7. Non-functional requirements

-   Odoo 17 ORM conventions; no direct SQL writes.
-   Multi-company and company-currency correctness.
-   Currency rounding through `res.currency`.
-   Server-side access checks.
-   Chatter tracking for critical fields and lifecycle events.
-   Transactional consistency and database constraints where possible.
-   Idempotent scheduled jobs and batch processing.
-   No N+1 queries in dashboards/reports.
-   Translation-ready Thai and English.
-   Secure attachments and QR routes.
-   Responsive UI using standard Odoo 17 web views.
-   Automated tests for business logic, access and accounting.
-   Upgrade-safe extension; no monkey-patching core methods.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

### Registration

-   [ ] Manual asset with unique code and required evidence.
-   [ ] Asset created from posted Vendor Bill line.
-   [ ] Split one bill line across multiple assets without
    over-allocation.
-   [ ] Duplicate allocation rejected.
-   [ ] Company/currency/account consistency enforced.

### Depreciation

-   [ ] Straight-line schedule totals depreciable base within currency
    rounding.
-   [ ] Final line absorbs rounding remainder.
-   [ ] First/last period rules match approved policy.
-   [ ] Posting creates balanced move with correct accounts.
-   [ ] Re-running cron/action does not duplicate move.
-   [ ] Locked accounting date blocks posting without bypass.
-   [ ] Reversal preserves original move and traceability.

### Operations

-   [ ] Transfer preserves old/new custody and location history.
-   [ ] QR scan opens authorized asset.
-   [ ] Audit snapshots expected data and records exceptions.
-   [ ] Maintenance does not silently capitalize cost.
-   [ ] Warranty reminders respect configured dates.

### Disposal and reports

-   [ ] Disposal stops future depreciation.
-   [ ] Sale proceeds are not double-posted.
-   [ ] Gain/loss agrees with disposal calculation.
-   [ ] Roll-forward and GL reconciliation expose variances.
-   [ ] Multi-company users cannot access unauthorized assets.

### Quality

-   [ ] Unit, model, access and tour tests pass.
-   [ ] Install, upgrade and uninstall behavior documented.
-   [ ] No legacy `attrs` / `states` view syntax.
-   [ ] No posted move is deleted by module code.

## 9. Delivery plan

**Phase 1 --- Foundation and accounting:** skeleton, security,
categories, register, Vendor Bill/manual registration, backend adapter,
depreciation, posting/reversal, disposal, core reports and tests.

**Phase 2 --- Operations:** transfers, employee/department/location, QR
labels, mobile scanning, maintenance, warranty, audit campaigns and
exception handling.

**Phase 3 --- Analytics and hardening:** dashboard, roll-forward, GL
reconciliation, performance, access and multi-company tests, UAT, import
and deployment runbook.

## 10. Decisions requiring sign-off before production

1.  Is Odoo 17 Enterprise `account_asset` installed, or is this
    Community/custom asset accounting?
2.  If installed, confirm native `account.asset` is the accounting
    backend. Extend via adapter; do not duplicate depreciation logic.
3.  Confirm monthly depreciation convention: full month, prorata
    temporis, half-month or company policy.
4.  Confirm capitalization threshold and eligible account policy.
5.  Confirm opening assets require opening journal entries or
    register-only migration.
6.  Confirm disposal gain/loss accounts and invoice linkage policy.
7.  Confirm whether inter-company transfers are required.

Use conservative defaults and block ambiguous accounting actions rather
than guessing.
